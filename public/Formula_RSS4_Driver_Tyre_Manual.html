🎮 How to Activate: The P2P system is bound to the KERS button in Assetto Corsa. Go to Controls > Car > KERS (or in Content Manager: Settings > Assetto Corsa > Controls > Patch > KERS) and map a button on your steering wheel. Pressing the button engages overboost instantly at full throttle.

⚠️ Critical Notice: Tyre Degradation & Explosion Hazard

Do not attempt to drive past tyre life! In Assetto Corsa, tyres do not simply wear down to lower grip—they will suffer catastrophic structural carcass explosions when pushed past their physical limit.

When the tyre reaches the end of its life and you experience a massive loss of grip, you have approximately 1 to 2 laps before an explosion could happen.

  • An explosion results in immediate deflation (0% grip), dropping the chassis onto the wheel rim.
  • This causes violent high-speed spinouts with total loss of steering and braking control.
🛡️
Golden Rule: When you feel that sudden, massive drop in grip, BOX IMMEDIATELY. Pushing for another lap or two carries an extreme risk of an unavoidable DNF.
